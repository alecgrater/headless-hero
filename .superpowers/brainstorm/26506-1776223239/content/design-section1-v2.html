Revised Pillow Implementation
  1. Remove solid colored outline (Layer 5) and energy ring (Layer 5b) entirely
  2. Wide outer glow: Radial gradient ellipse extending ~25px past circle edge, white with subtle per-circle color tint (derived from segment color), heavily Gaussian blurred (~20px)
  3. Inner vignette: After pasting the circle image, overlay a radial gradient mask — transparent center, darkening to 50-60% black at edges. This makes the image look "inside" a sphere.
  4. Specular highlight: Semi-transparent white ellipse on upper portion of circle, blurred to simulate light bounce on glass surface
  5. Tint color: Each circle's glow gets a very subtle color derived from its circle_color (desaturated, lightened) — mostly white with just a hint

Closer to the reference now? The key changes: no hard borders, wider glow, dark vignette inside, no rainbow effect.
