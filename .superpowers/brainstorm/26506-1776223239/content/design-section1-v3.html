Pillow Implementation — Revised
  1. Bright core ring: Draw a white/cyan circle outline (~4-5px width, high alpha ~170) at circle edge. This is the bright visible border.
  2. Inner glow: Duplicate the ring, Gaussian blur ~12px inward. Creates the glow bleeding into the image.
  3. Outer glow: Duplicate the ring, Gaussian blur ~20-25px outward. Creates the wide magical emanation.
  4. Second outer bloom: Even wider, softer pass (~45px blur, low alpha) for the furthest glow reach.
  5. Inner vignette: Radial gradient mask on the circle image — transparent center, darkening at edges.
  6. Top specular: Small white ellipse highlight on upper portion.
  7. Tint: Ring color is mostly white (220,245,255) with a very subtle per-circle tint derived from segment color.

The key insight I was missing: the ring itself is visible and bright — it's not invisible ambient glow, it's a distinct glowing circle outline with bloom spreading from it.
