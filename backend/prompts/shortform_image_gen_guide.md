# Short-Form Image Generation Style Guide

Generate images optimized for vertical mobile viewing (9:16 portrait format).

## Composition Rules
- **Vertical framing only** — all subjects composed for tall, narrow frame
- **Single focal point** — one dominant subject, centered or slightly above center
- **Close-up to medium shots** — faces, objects, and details fill the frame
- **No horizontal panoramas** — avoid wide establishing shots or landscapes
- **Top-third and bottom-third clear** — leave space for text overlays and subtitles

## Visual Style
- **High contrast** — bold darks and bright highlights for small-screen readability
- **Saturated colors** — pop on mobile screens; avoid muted or pastel tones
- **Cinematic lighting** — dramatic rim lighting, strong key light, deep shadows
- **Clean backgrounds** — dark or blurred backgrounds isolate the subject
- **Large visual elements** — nothing small or intricate; everything reads at phone size

## Technical
- Dark void or gradient backgrounds preferred (matches dark-mode UI)
- Neon accent colors for energy and contrast
- Smooth, premium textures — no noise, grain, or artifacts
- 2.5D aesthetic with depth — foreground subject pops from background
